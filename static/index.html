Hues & Cues Rules

Objective

Give one- and two-word clues to guide guessers toward a specific color on the 480-square board.

Clue Restrictions

  • No basic color names (red, blue, green, yellow, orange, purple, pink, brown, white, black, grey).
  • No board coordinates (e.g. "K15") or positional references ("top", "middle").
  • No room items.
  • Cue 1: exactly 1 word. Cue 2: at most 2 words (cannot use "lighter" or "darker").

Scoring

  • Exact square: 3 points.
  • Inside 3×3 frame: 2 points.
  • Outer 5×5 boundary: 1 point.
  • Cue Giver scores 1 point per guess inside the 3×3 frame (2 points in 3-player games). Max 9.
